Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Gravity Pop! ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application across iOS and Android devices.

Key Summary: Gravity Pop! does NOT require account registration and does NOT collect your personal name, physical address, email address, or phone number. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

When you play Gravity Pop!, certain non-personal device information may be processed locally or by trusted third-party service partners to operate the game:

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: High scores, unlocked themes, coin balances, and achievements are stored locally on your device using standard secure mobile storage.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), screen resolution, preferred language, and anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs and maintain smooth 60 FPS performance.
  • Motion Sensor Data: Device tilt (gyroscope/accelerometer) is processed strictly in real-time in local memory to calculate gravity physics. Sensor data is never saved, transmitted, or logged.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Gravity Pop! is a free-to-play game supported by third-party mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Google AdMob may collect and process device-specific identifiers (such as Apple’s IDFA on iOS with user permission, or Google Advertising ID on Android) and IP addresses to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms: How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps.

4. Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On devices running iOS 14.5 and later, we respect Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. We request your explicit permission before any advertising identifier is accessed. If you choose "Ask App not to Track", you will still receive standard non-personalized ads, and no cross-app tracking occurs.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Gravity Pop! is designed as a family-friendly arcade puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has inadvertently provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove such data.

6. In-App Purchases & Virtual Items

If you purchase the optional "Remove Ads / VIP Pass" or coin bundles, financial transactions are processed securely and directly by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). We never receive or store your credit card or billing details.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Opt-Out Options

You have full control over your advertising data through your device settings:

  • iOS: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and toggle tracking off for Gravity Pop!.
  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Reset Game Data: You can reset all local progress at any time by clearing app data or reinstalling the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling in Gravity Pop!, please reach out to us at:
